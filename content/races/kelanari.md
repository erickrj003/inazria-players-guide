## Kelanari

The Kelanari are the people of the Violet Sea itself: the great inland ocean whose currents bind Kystriland, Inazria, and Avarazhan into one revolving wheel of life. Kelanari society is among the oldest civilizations in Azergad. Its cities are grown and carved into living reef and sheer sea cliff, their flowing halls shaped so that the currents themselves move through them like breath. Kelanari highly value the arts, which are made to be experienced in the water. Their sculptures are carved at the surface to sing as the current passes over it beside floating settlements. The coral courts of Azimar, the capital city and meeting place of the nations, have hosted the embassies of every Realm. Yet, the Kelanari crown jewel of Azimar remains to be seen by most outsiders.

The Kelanari's famous political neutrality is longstanding tradition spanning thousands of years. When their Paragon, Calrien the High Shepherd, signed the Great Pact on behalf of the Violet Sea, the Kelanari inherited a peculiar position. Just as the commerce of every Realm crosses their waters, so too would their conflicts. The Courts of Corrindan resolved that the sea would belong to no alliance, harbor no navy, and take no side. Even as a culture, most Kelanari choose not to interfere with the quarrels of land-dwelling men unless the Sea itself is threatened. In the same spirit, Calrien identifies most men as neither friend nor foe, roaming and defending the wild reaches of Alore with her Shepherds. As part of their contribution to the Pact, Kelanari construct and maintain great lighthouses spanning the well-charted Violet Sea.

Kelanari people are lean and graceful, standing between 5 and 6 feet in height, with narrow, muscular builds adapted for swimming. Their skin ranges from deep blue-green to pale aquamarine, and includes subtle scale patterns along their arms and legs. Distinctive gill slits along their necks close when on land, and their webbed fingers and toes make them exceptional swimmers. Their large, glowing eyes are adapted for underwater vision and feature shades of deep blue, green, or silver irises atop fully black sclera. Most Kelanari have flowing hair in blues, greens, or silver-white that moves like kelp in the water.

Kelanari people are capable of living lives reaching 250 years or longer. However, like food, water and rest, a Kelanari dwelling on land needs to regularly revitalize their bodies by immersing fully underwater. Typically, Kelanari choose to submerge fully while they sleep. Kelanari who forgo this for even a few days are highly prone to shumza, a terrible and painful disease that causes Kelanari skin and organs to dry up, flake and erode. Feeling the effects of shumza even once can drastically reduce a Kelanari's lifespan.

Generally speaking, their long lives grant the Kelanari patience and wisdom. Kelanari are naturally level-headed and calm, valuing passivity and patience even in the face of overwhelming crisis. Though Dwarves and Humans sometimes grow impatient with them, the Kelanari view land-dwellers only with caution rather than coldness. Though they understanding the value of outside contact, Kelanari prefer the safe domain of their underwater kingdoms. The few Kelanari that take to the roads of the surface world are the curious and the called: traders, rescuers, singers gathering the songs of the land, and the occasional envoy of the coral courts sworn to secrecy.

### Traits

- **Ability Score Increase**: +2 Constitution
- **Age**: Kelanari mature by age 30 and can over 250 years long. To maintain your health, you must fully submerge your body for at least 8 hours in sea or sea-like water once every 3 days (Limnari characters must use fresh water instead).
- **Size**: Kelanari stand between 5 and 6 feet tall, with lean, muscular frames. Your size is Medium.
- **Speed**: Your base walking speed is 25 feet. You have a swimming speed of 40 feet.
- **Amphibious**: You can freely breathe both air and water.
- **Darkvision**: Adapted to the ocean depths, you can see in dim light within 60 feet as if it were bright light, and darkness as if it were dim light, but only in shades of blue, green and purple.
- **Natural Swimmer**: Always add your proficiency bonus when you make a Strength (Athletics) check to swim, dive, or stay afloat.
- **Aquatic Sense**: You are naturally sensitive to the water and the air pressure around you. You always know your depth below the surface and the direction to open air. You can sense the direction and distance of bodies of water as small as 200 gallons full anywhere within a 1-mile radius.
- **Kelanari Soak**: When you finish a long rest fully submerged in sea or sea-like water, you gain additional temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum of 1). If you miss a soak for 24 hours, you immediately lose any temporary hit points you have from this ability and gain one level of exhaustion. This exhaustion level is removed when you finish a long rest fully submerged once again.
- **Languages**: You can speak, read, and write Common and Kelanari. Kelanari is a flowing, musical language that incorporates tonal elements mimicking ocean currents and the songs of other sea creatures.

### Kaduran

Peaceful, diplomatic and adventurous, Kaduran are common Kelanari often seen on land living full lives beside the other races of men. They are master craftspeople, known widely for creating beautiful and intricate jewelry and sculptures made with coral and pearls, which are highly prized all around Azergad. Kaduran make their homes in sea caves and tidal pools around the smaller Kystriland islands and across the shore on the mainland. Larger settlements are semi-aquatic, frequently found upon shallow places like islands or sand bars, where tide pools and constructed channels bring the sea directly into their homes.

Land traders tend to remember the warm Kaduran welcome and easygoing, generous style of business. Most Kaduran know enough Kystri to make strangers feel at peace even among their strange villages. Kaduran people are driven to trade for Human herbs, spices and coffee, which have become very popular all across the Violet Sea.

Generations of sun and salt have left them with warm teal and copper-colored skin, especially across their shoulders, arms, hands and head. Kaduran stand at about the same height as other Kelanari, but are a bit lean and weathered. Their hair is often cut short for work ashore, and their eyes subtly glow green, amber, or sea-gray.

- **Ability Score Increase**: +1 Charisma
- **Language**: You can speak Kadurani, a mutually-intelligible dialect of Kelanari which incorporates many Kystri trade terms. Kaduran with an Intelligence score of 13 or higher are also fluent in basic Kystri.
- **Coral Crafting**: You have proficiency with jeweler's tools. Whenever you make an ability check to craft, repair, or appraise items made of coral, pearl, shell, or other sea-grown materials, you add double your proficiency bonus to the check, instead of your normal proficiency bonus.
- **Tidal Attunement**: Water and mud no deeper than 5 feet is not difficult terrain for you. You always know the current state of the tide and whether it is rising or falling. By studying a stretch of coastline for one minute, you can identify the safest places to land, anchor, or shelter a vessel, as well as any hazards hidden beneath the waterline.

### Pelagoran

The Pelagoran live aboard great floating cities, built upon well-constructed rafts and organic hulls, that drift with the currents of the Violet Sea. These nomadic Kelanari are master navigators, capable of guiding their settlements away from areas they predict will be hit by storms, in order to both protect themselves and follow the wildlife for easier hunting. The Pelagoran are a carefree and boisterous people. They aren't particularly business-oriented, and prefer to guide their structures far away from the shoreline where their lifestyle can continue unimpeded. To visitors, they lack a certain degree of personal space and manners, but are otherwise are charming and excitable by simple things.

Pelagoran communities are famous for their Illelan: epic songs which are sung by entire communities in unison that can be heard for miles across the water. Each Pelagoran community writes at least one and recites it on a regular basis (as per Pelagoran tradition). An Illelan is sometimes used for communication with other communities and to guide lost travelers to safety.

Pelagoran people share a common Kelanari frame but with broader shoulders and deep blue skin. Their hair is often very long and untamed. Pelagoran eyes are uniquely good at seeing very far distances, glowing brightly in many shades of blue, green and silver.

- **Ability Score Increase**: +1 Wisdom
- **Language**: You can speak Pelagorani, a highly unique language relying on various tones and clicks. It is common for different Pelagoran groups to have many differences in their language; in fact, it is thought that each floating Pelagoran community speaks a unique dialect. Due to the many sounds required, it is a difficult language for non-Kelanari to learn.
- **Horizon Watcher**: On open water, rain, glare, and mist do not impose disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks you make to spot vessels, land, or weather. You also know the distance to anything you can see on the water.
- **Master Navigator**: You have proficiency with navigator's tools and can never become lost at sea, barring magical effects. You have advantage on Survival checks made at sea.

### Khemazi

The mysterious Khemazi are a reserved, calculated, and somewhat inflexible people that dwell in the darkest depths of the Violet Sea in cities built within huge trenches and abyssal plains. These Kelanari have developed unique senses, adapted to the crushing depths and eternal darkness. Khemazi, a word that means "High Kelanari", believe that their rich and distinguished society sets them apart from the other Kelanari peoples. They remain impartial to land-dwellers, generally. 

The great city of Zerakahm, the largest and grandest of Kelanari cities, is raised upon ruins as old as Azergad itself. These drowned remains of the first Kelanari settlements, sunk into the deep in an upheaval that far predates the Great Pact and its records, are said to contain vast amounts of hidden knowledge. The Khemazi are a scholarly people in their own right, and consider themselves heirs to the history found among those original cities. The greatest among them are tasked with perfect preservation of the written contents and safeguarding it forever. Even the Courts of Corrindan ask the Khemazi for answers rather than permission to look.

Khemazi are physically distinct from other Kelanari. They are shorter and denser on average, with very dark skin and very little hair on their bodies. Their eyes are even larger than normal and glow quite brightly, adapted well for their sunless domain. Due to the great distance from their homes, and because they lack the same sense for bodies of water as other Kelenari have, Khemazi rarely travel to land without a particularly compelling reason.

- **Ability Score Increase**: +1 Intelligence
- **Language**: You can speak Khemazi, the ancient language from which modern Kelanari derives. To outsiders, the language is poetic and formal.
- **Abyssal Adaptation**: You can survive at any depth. Whenever you make an Intelligence (History) or Intelligence (Arcana) check about the deep ocean, sunken ruins, or events from before the Great Pact, you are considered proficient in the skill and add double your proficiency bonus to the check, instead of your normal proficiency bonus.
- **Deep Sight**: Your darkvision extends to 90 feet, and you can see normally in complete darkness. You have disadvantage on checks related to the effects of bright lights.
- **Lack of Aquatic Sense**: Khemazi do not benefit from Aquatic Sense.

### Limnari

Far from the Violet Sea, in the freshwater lakes and slow rivers scattered across Zilwynbar and northern Azelliot, live the Limnari: the curious case of a Kelanari people found outside the sea itself. The Limnari are highly distinct from their cousins in the Violet Sea. Their relaxed attitudes combined with their simple villages within ponds, lakes and streams (standing quiet against the grandeur of Violet Sea cities) cause other Kelanari to half-jokingly question their kinship altogether. They are fishers, potters, and painters who primarily trade lake-sourced goods with the neighboring Lendolo, Aaldegar and Wendric peoples. 

Yet, beneath the easy surface runs an ancient, solemn duty. The lakes of the Limnari open downward into the sunless, treacherous waterways that vein the earth below Azelliot and Zilwynbar - the same hidden waters credited for the astonishing fertility of both lands. It's from down below that indescribable, horrible creatures are said to dwell, yet the surface world knows little of such things — and the Limnari keep it that way. The Limnari consider it their sacred duty to guard the surface world from such monstrous beasts. 

Limnari skin runs in soft olive, river-green, and silver-brown tones, brightest along the arms, and their eyes are warm amber, green, or brown; their hair is typically dark, worn loose in the water and bound with painted reeds and seaweed on land.

- **Ability Score Increase**: +1 Strength
- **Language**: You can speak Limnari, a slow, warm dialect of Kelanari full of lake-specific language and loanwords from Lendolo and Halfling. It keeps the tonal rises of Pelagorani, but takes its unhurried pace from the still water it was made beside.
- **Delver of the Underlakes**: Generations of tending the drowned passages beneath the lakes have made their darkness familiar. While underwater or within flooded caves and tunnels, you cannot become lost except by magical effects.
- **Freshwater Dweller**: You have proficiency in the Nature skill. You know whether fresh water you can see or touch is safe to drink or breathe. When you make an Intelligence (Nature) check about a creature that lives in fresh water, you add double your proficiency bonus to the check, instead of your normal proficiency bonus.
- **Salt Strain**: Your gills and skin are tuned to fresh water. While swimming in salt water, your swimming speed is reduced by 10 feet. You cannot breath in salt water, and it does not count when determining your Kelanari-specific needs (you must use fresh water instead).
