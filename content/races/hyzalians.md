## Hyzalians

The Hyzalians are the elder people of the Marram: a people which is careful, wise, and cloistered across the unforgiving snowcapped summits. They value tradition and deep understanding, reciting the wisdom of the past before deciding on the future. To the wider Realms, they are an unusual and ominous bunch: a people without a Paragon, living above the Pact's roads, whose long memories outlast the politics of the modern day.

Ancient Hyzalian literature is of a distinctly religious nature. According to Inazrian mythology, they are said to be the original descendants of Tamid'Zelnah, the being which first unified men in mind and spirit. However, the Hyzalians were cursed: a perfect creation cast down to mortality and left to struggle for themselves. Although a handful of Hyzalians truly devote themselves to this ancient religion today, their numbers are few and dwindling.

History has given the Hyzalians reason for their caution. Alone among the races of man, they have no Paragon, and the Marram was initially set apart from the Great Pact by the Marram Covenant. Officially, this was seen as a move made out of reverence, but as time has passed few Hyzalians tell it that way. Due to the way historical events have unfolded, the world's grief hardened into a suspicion of all Hyzalians that has never fully thawed. Famously, it was Karhu's campaign that drove the Hyzal people from the lower slopes; the Treaty of Marram (890 1G) that ended it divided those slopes between Inazria and Bisengal, granting the Hyzalians unwanted citizenship on paper. Without much consultation, families and whole communities were split by arbitrary borders. Hyzalians divided themselves by beliefs, perceptions, and hopes of better lives in their respective new Realms. Ultimately, the mass disruption caused by the Treaty of Marram resulted in a fractured identity that has left the Hyzalian people poor and more isolated than ever. Both in Inazria and Bisengal, Hyzalians find themselves treated as lower-class citizens in one way or another. Some Hyzalians fled their homes for new Realms entirely, seeking a better life away from the chaos. Generations-old squabbles from the division persist to this day.

It is no surprise, then, that Hyzalians overwhelmingly view outsiders with distrust. To start, outsider civilization has perverted and twisted their concepts of morality to be something deeply unrecognizable. Hyzalian culture is full of rituals and living historical practices that outsiders wouldn't understand. Honoring the many holidays and traditional practices can take weeks to months out of the year, and the slow pace of Hyzalian life leaves little time for much else. Moreso, it's also an incredibly communal society: Hyzalian tradition allows no individual or family ownership of property or essentials, because these things are owned and used collectively as a village. A Hyzalian member of society must work to support their village first. When villages become too large to support organically, the practice of establishing a new village occurs: a group of settlers embark on a day's journey away from the old village, until a suitable valley is found by nightfall.

Daily life on the peaks is quiet, ordered, and built against the cold. Hyzalian cloisters are carved into ridgelines and icy hollows. Long stone halls are built with low doors and long tables for the elders to speak. Meals are simple, sustaining, and a little bland: barley and mountain greens, dried meat, and a fermented milk drink called Salli. Hyzalian crafts are rugged and practical. Though guests are highly unusual, it should be known that speaking first, especially over an elder, will set outsiders up for failure. 

The few Hyzal that venture away from their homes often return with sad stories of misguided, impulsive, self-absorbed, and untrustworthy men. On the flip side, the other races perceive Hyzalian culture as primitive, rigid, and alien. Hyzalians are also said to have extremely long memories. They are also not known to forgive easily. The rare Hyzalian traveller on the roads of Azergad is walking through a world that seems to have forgotten them: perhaps they have left their home to trade, study, or search out kin scattered by the passage of time. Though earning their trust is a trial of its own, a Hyzalian companion is a lifelong ally.

Hyzalians are elegant creatures. Men are around the same height as humans, while women tend to be a few inches shorter. Hyzalian skin is very thick and lustrous like metal. Skin tones are various shades of gray: light and dark, and sometimes with brown or bluish tints depending on the family. Their maw is large, holding a grand set of teeth including two exaggerated upper canines. Their ears protrude from the skull diagonally and feature circular shapes. Their eyes are uniquely adapted to their world: they are quite large, and the external eyelids protect an interior set of semi-transparent eyelids that close horizontally. The underlying eyes burst brilliantly with colors: most commonly, patterns feature shades of brown, yellow, red, light blue and violet. Healthy males are heavy, and weigh between 250 and 400 pounds on average, while women are typically 50 to 60 pounds less. Hyzalians reach adulthood at age 25 and commonly live beyond 500 years. Hyzalians are famously known for their thick, bright white hair that grows like a mane around their heads. While soft, it is exceptionally durable and difficult to cut. As per tradition, Hyzalians do not ever cut their hair. It grows until the Hyzalian reaches adulthood and, once cut, can never grow back. A Hyzalian with very long hair is held in very high esteem, while a shaved Hyzalian is shameful and not to be trusted.

Te-Hyzalians trace their ancestry to Te, who is said to be the first Hyzalian to discover darkness. Extremely little is known about them apart from stories and folklore. A living Te-Hyzalian encounter has not been documented for many centuries, though rumors and tales of their presence linger on.

### Traits

- **Ability Score Increase**: +1 Wisdom, +1 Constitution. Hyzalians naturally desire understanding and truth, while their tough constitutions carry them through the harshest environments in the world. Upon reaching level 10, you may choose any ability score and increase it by 1.
- **Age**: Hyzalians reach adulthood at age 25 and commonly live beyond 500 years.
- **Size**: Hyzalians are broad and imposing. Men stand about the height of humans and weigh between 250 and 400 pounds. Women are typically a few inches shorter and 50 to 60 pounds lighter. Your size is Medium.
- **Speed**: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Darkvision**: You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light, and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can't discern colors in darkness.
- **Marram Fortitude**: Hyzalians possess impressive athletic bodies. You have proficiency in the Athletics skill, and you are acclimated to high altitude and extreme cold.
- **Thick Skin**: Hyzalian skin is difficult to penetrate and acts as a form of natural protection. When you take slashing or piercing damage, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage by your proficiency bonus. Once you use this trait, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
- **Glare Resistance**: Hyzalians have adapted to a bright, snowy environment above the clouds. You have advantage on saving throws against being blinded. Bright lights or flashes that would cause other creatures to struggle seeing through do not effect Hyzalians.
- **Languages**: You can speak, read, and write Common and Hyzalian. The Hyzalian language combines physical gestures with a variety of sounds, but is highly expressive and rich in vocabulary. It shares some of its history, including a script, some phrases, and concepts, with Inazri. Virtually no outsiders ever fully understand it. Among their own peaks, many Hyzalians live whole lives without needing Common, but it is taught in order to handle confrontations with outsiders.
