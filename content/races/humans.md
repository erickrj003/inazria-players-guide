## Humans

The Humans of Inazria are perhaps the most diverse and adaptable of all the races, spread across the tropical peninsula from the great capital of Thilidan to the remote fishing villages along the coast. The Human civilization has grown to include a huge variety of cultures and practices. Human society is marked by its rapid pace of change and remarkable capacity for both great achievement and devastating conflict. The human race truly exists everywhere — even in the most remote, extreme, or unlikely places in the world.

Humans are the largest race in Azergad by population, and their shorter lifespans create a sense of urgency that drives them to accomplish great things in relatively brief periods. They are natural traders and diplomats, serving as bridges between the other races through their adaptability and openness to new ideas. Human settlements range from the sprawling metropolis of Thilidan to small farming communities, each reflecting a local culture and environment of its own. The young of Inazria sign onto trading ships, caravan escorts, and adventuring companies as readily as they take up family trades like crafting and harvesting. Humans are proud of their accomplishments (and don't mind boasting of them).

Humans all over Inazria dress in long wrapped and layered cloth dyed in fierce colors, with jewelry at the ears and wrists for the wealthy.

The cities and towns of the peninsula are described in [Geography](../lore/geography.md#inazria).

Physically, humans are highly variable but typically stand between 5 and 6 and a half feet tall with all kinds of lean and stocky builds. Their skin tones vary from pale to deep brown, with hair colors spanning the full spectrum from black to blonde. Humans mature by age 18 and typically live 75–90 years, though some exceptional individuals may reach 100 and beyond.

### Traits

- **Ability Score Increase**: +1 to two different ability scores of your choice. At 10th level, you may add an additional +1 to any ability score.
- **Age**: Humans mature by age 18 and live up to 90 years.
- **Size**: Humans vary widely in height and build. Your size is Medium.
- **Speed**: Your base walking speed is 30 feet.
- **Versatile**: You gain proficiency in one skill of your choice.
- **Crossroads Upbringing**: Raised where every race of man passes through, you gain proficiency with one tool, gaming set, or musical instrument of your choice. Additionally, you can convey and understand simple ideas with any creature that speaks a language, using the gestures, loanwords, and market-pidgin of the great trade cities.
- **Determined**: You can add your proficiency bonus to one ability check that doesn't already include your proficiency bonus. You can use this trait once per long rest.
- **Languages**: You can speak, read, and write Common, plus your choice of trade language: Kystri, Veldtwin, or Wendric. Inazrian-born Humans are also exposed to Inazri, the classical Inazrian tongue, though full fluency is rare (see *Inazri* below).

### Inazri

Inazri is the classical tongue of the peoples of Inazria — the language that was spoken in Thilidan when the Pact was signed, and which was once the daily speech of the Inazrian peninsula. Today it survives as a ceremonial and literary register rather than a vernacular. It is the language of oaths, blessings, festival songs, religious recitations, and gravestone inscriptions; of family names, place names, and the names given to newborn children; and of formal documents, royal decrees, and certain ritual texts. Inazrian-born Humans grow up hearing Inazri at weddings, funerals, harvest festivals, and the naming of children, and most might recite a handful of phrases, but daily life is conducted in Common.

Fluent speakers of Inazri are uncommon. They are rural village elders, antiquarians, scholars, priests, and the senior officers of certain Inazrian institutions. To be a fluent reader of old Inazri is to belong to a small society of those who can interpret what was originally written when the world was younger.

A Human character whose background includes a rural Inazrian upbringing, formal religious or scholarly training, or service to a Paragon's seat may, at the GM's discretion, gain the ability to speak, read, and write Inazri as an additional language. Inazrian Humans of any background may freely use Inazri-rooted personal names, family names, and place names regardless of fluency.
