Rindan
Vailoko Nafazi
Andasena

Rindan's story begins on the distant, whimsical island of Andasena. The son of tea gardeners, Rindan Tariandi was the 7th of 9 children; by all accounts, his family was as typical as Vailoko families get. The most remarkable (and often gloated about) feat of the Tariandi clan was performed by Rindan's cousin, Zaja: he was crowned Kamonganu, a lifelong name and status symbol given to the winner of the great waterfall climbing contest. 

Rindan showed an innate talent for magic at a very young age. On his home island of Pelgur (within the Senawara lake system), Rindan began discovering how to control his magical talents on his own. Even by the 4th Generation, the Vailoko knew little about magecraft: due to the extreme remote nature of Andasena, no true, scholarly wizard from any of Azergad's Magi schools had visited them. During Rindan's youth, only a handful of Vailoko elders had taken even a limited study of the practice. 

Rindan began discovering practical applications of his skills when he was just 8 years old. On the night of his birthday, he unintentionally caused his robes to glow. In fact, they began glowing brighter and brighter until it became unbearable. Unsure of how to reverse his spell, Rindan was forced to tear his robe and stuff it in a trunk, where it remains to this day.

Shortly after, Rindan discovered his knack for teleportation. It started by sending island squirrels from one tree to another, before quickly turning into pranks on his siblings. He kept this particular magic a secret. On a serene Andasena evening, Rindan felt ready to experiment on himself. He wanted to know, "How far could I go?" In a flash, Rindan's whole life was warped: after experiencing the uncomfortable, soul-ripping pain of teleportation, his careless mistake wound up inserting himself in a vastly unfamiliar world. When the boy emerged, he was no longer surrounded by temperate forests and lakes. He was no longer on Andasena at all. Instead, the boy had landed in the homeland of the Dalvas: the great wide plains of Azelliot. 

Suffice to say, Rindan Tariandi would not be returning home anytime soon.
