## Ascetic — Path to Perfection

In Inazria, Ascetics walk a disciplined path toward mortal perfectionism, pursuing ancient philosophical traditions tied to cultural or ethnic heritage. Through unwavering focus and precise execution, Ascetics maintain their Flow to produce extraordinary feats. One misstep can shatter their rhythm, forcing them back to square one. As with all classes in Inazria, your context supplies most of your identity and mechanics; the base class provides the core engine.

An Ascetic's training is inseparable from their tradition's temple: a cloistered community of fellow practitioners, bound by shared discipline as much as by shared technique. You train among other Ascetics of your context — Tammuzian acolytes in their fire-temples, Nityamar monks at the world's most hostile extremes, Khalsari brothers in their border cloisters — often for years before anyone considers you ready to walk the road alone. The Nehushtani alone keep no temple at all; their tradition passes from a single elder to a single apprentice, in secret, one lifetime at a time. A character may only ever choose one primal class and one Ascetic context, for life. Discuss the origin of your training with your GM; typically, you take this class at 1st level. You may multiclass freely into any civil class thereafter.

### Hit Points
- **Hit Dice**: 1d8 per ascetic level
- **Hit Points at 1st Level**: 8 + your Constitution modifier
- **Hit Points at Higher Levels**: 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier per ascetic level after 1st

### Proficiencies
- **Armor**: Light armor
- **Weapons**: Simple weapons
- **Tools**: Choose one type of artisan's tools or one musical instrument
- **Saving Throws**: Dexterity, Strength
- **Skills**: Choose two from Acrobatics, Athletics, History, Insight, Religion, Stealth

### Equipment
You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:
- (a) a quarterstaff or (b) any simple weapon
- (a) a dungeoneer's pack or (b) an explorer's pack
- 10 darts

### Class Rules
- **Ongoing Features and Concentration**. If a feature creates a lasting zone, form, or summoned ally, you concentrate on it as if concentrating on a spell, unless the feature says otherwise.
- **Once on Each of Your Turns**. When a feature could trigger multiple times in a round (such as an aura or additional effect), it can do so no more than once on each of your turns unless the feature says otherwise.
- **Limited Uses**. If a feature has a limited number of uses and doesn't specify a recharge, you can use it a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

### Core Class Features
- **The Flowing Path (1st Level)**. You pursue perfection through precise execution. Your performance in combat is measured by your Flow, which ranges from -3 (Disrupted) to +3 (Flawless). You begin each day at 0 Flow.
  - **You gain 1 Flow boost when you:**
    - Score a critical hit with an attack roll
    - Cause an attack against you to miss (including attacks you impose disadvantage upon or cause to miss through abilities)
    - Succeed on a saving throw by 5 or more
    - Reduce a creature to less than 1 hit point
  - **You lose 1 Flow boost when:**
    - You fail a saving throw
    - You take damage that remains equal to or greater than your proficiency bonus after applying resistances, immunities, and temporary hit points — a scratch doesn't break your rhythm, but a real blow does. You can lose Flow this way only once per turn, no matter how many separate instances of damage you take.
    - You make an attack roll and deal no damage by the end of your turn
  - You can have a maximum of +3 Flow. If you would gain Flow above +3, your Flow remains at +3. Your Flow can drop as low as -3. When your Flow is at -1 or lower, you are **Disrupted**. When your Flow is at +3, you are **Flawless**.
- **Unarmored Defense (1st Level)**. While you are wearing no armor and not wielding a shield, your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier + your Wisdom modifier. (Some contexts substitute a different ability score for this second modifier; if so, that context says so explicitly.) If you are Disrupted (Flow at -1 or lower), your AC equals 10 + your Dexterity modifier only.
- **Flow Techniques (1st Level)**. While Flowing (0 or higher Flow), you can spend Flow to execute precise techniques. You can spend Flow immediately when you gain a Flow boost, or on your turn as part of another action. While Disrupted, you cannot use Flow techniques.
  - **Flowing Strike** (1 Flow, immediate when you score a critical hit). You add your Wisdom modifier to the damage roll and choose one of the following effects: knock the target prone, push the target 10 feet away from you, or cause the target to bleed (it takes 1d4 damage at the start of each of its turns for 1 minute; the target or a creature within 5 feet of it can use an action to stanch the wound, ending the effect).
  - **Flowing Counter** (2 Flow, immediate reaction when an attack against you misses). You make one weapon attack against the attacker if it is within your reach. This attack scores a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20.
  - **Flowing Maneuver** (1 Flow, on your turn). You move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks. You can move through spaces occupied by hostile creatures during this movement.
- **Disciplined Movement (2nd Level)**. Your speed increases by 10 feet while you are Flowing (0 or higher Flow) and you are not wearing armor or wielding a shield. This bonus increases when you reach certain ascetic levels: to 15 feet at 6th level, 20 feet at 10th level, 25 feet at 14th level, and 30 feet at 18th level. If you are Disrupted, you do not gain this speed bonus.
- **Find Your Flow (2nd Level)**. You can use a bonus action to focus your mind and body. Until the start of your next turn, you have advantage on attack rolls, and each time you hit with an attack or an attack against you misses, you gain 1 Flow boost. If you are Disrupted when you use this feature, you gain at most 1 Flow boost from it in total, no matter how many times you qualify — the rhythm returns in fits, not all at once. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
- **Martial Precision (2nd Level)**. You can use Dexterity instead of Strength for the attack and damage rolls of simple weapons.
- **Ability Score Improvement (4th, 8th, 12th, 16th, 19th Levels)**. Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 (to a maximum of 20).
- **Extra Attack (5th Level)**. When you take the Attack action on your turn, you can attack twice instead of once. If you are Flawless (Flow at +3) when you take the Attack action, you can make three attacks instead of two.
- **Mental Fortress (7th Level)**. While you are Flowing (0 or higher Flow), you can't be charmed or frightened.
- **Calm Mind (7th Level)**. You can use your action to end one condition on yourself (charmed, frightened, stunned, or paralyzed) and gain 1 Flow boost. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
- **Reactive Mastery (11th Level)**. Your instincts sharpen beyond ordinary reflex. You can take one additional reaction each round, for two reactions total. This additional reaction can only be used for Flow techniques or abilities granted by your context. A context's capstone feature is the only kind of feature that could ever grant you a further reaction beyond this one — none of the traditions below do, and no context stacks reactions to three or four per round.
- **Flowing Deflection (11th Level)**. When you are hit by a ranged weapon attack, you can use your reaction to spend 1 Flow and deflect the projectile. The damage you take from the attack is reduced by 1d10 + your Dexterity modifier + your ascetic level. If you reduce the damage to 0, you can catch the projectile if you have a free hand.
- **Transcendent Form (15th Level)**. You no longer age, you require no food or water, and you are immune to disease and poison. While you are Flawless (Flow at +3), you regain hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier at the start of each of your turns.
- **Flow Mastery (20th Level)**. Your Dexterity and Wisdom scores each increase by 2. Your maximum for those scores increases to 22. Your maximum Flow increases to +4. When you reach +4 Flow, you gain all benefits of being Flawless, and your critical hit range increases to 18-20. At the start of your turn, if your Flow is 0 or lower, you gain 1 Flow boost.

## Flawless State Benefits
When your Flow is at +3 (or +4 at 20th level), you are Flawless. You gain the following benefits:
- Your critical hit range increases by 1 (you score a critical hit on a roll of 19 or 20)
- You gain a +2 bonus to AC
- You have advantage on initiative rolls
- You can make three attacks instead of two when you take the Attack action (from Extra Attack)
- At 20th level, when you reach +4 Flow, your critical hit range becomes 18-20

## Disrupted State Limitations
When your Flow is at -1 or lower, you are Disrupted. You suffer the following limitations:
- You cannot use Flow techniques (you have no Flow to spend)
- Your Unarmored Defense becomes 10 + Dexterity modifier only (you lose the second ability modifier to your AC, whether that is Wisdom or a modifier your context substitutes for it)
- You do not gain the speed bonus from Disciplined Movement
- You do not gain the benefits of Mental Fortress (you can be charmed or frightened)
- You do not gain any benefits from being Flawless

While Disrupted, you still retain your base combat capabilities (attack rolls, damage, reactions, and Extra Attack). You must rely on solid fundamentals and the aid of your allies to climb back into positive Flow. Allies can use the Help action to grant you advantage on attacks, increasing your chance to hit and gain Flow boosts.

## Contexts

### Acolytes of Tammuzia (tah-moo-ZEE-ah) — Sacred Flame and Divine Light

The Tammuzians believe perfection is achieved through the purifying sacred flame and divine illumination. Acolytes of Tammuzia see fire as both literal and metaphorical—it cleanses impurity, reveals truth, and represents the divine presence within. Combat becomes a devotional dance: each movement a prayer, each strike an offering. Tammuzian power is controlled, sacred, and restorative. They are wandering mystics, healers, and warriors of light who move like flickering flames across the battlefield. Tammuzian communities cluster wherever fire burns brightest — active volcanoes, sweltering arid deserts — and their acolytes are drawn overwhelmingly from the Dalvas, Orcs, Cahokari, Keldale, and Guphati: peoples who made their peace with heat long before they made peace with the flame.

**Philosophy**: Purity through sacred flame; devotion through motion; healing through divine light.

**Flow Gain — Divine Favor**. You gain 1 Flow boost when you restore hit points to an ally (including yourself) with a spell or class feature (once per turn), or when you reduce a fiend or undead to 0 hit points.

**Flow Spend — Sacred Techniques**. You learn additional ways to spend Flow unique to your tradition:
- **Purifying Touch** (2 Flow, action or immediate on a critical roll). Touch one creature within 5 feet. If it's an ally, it regains hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier + your ascetic level and you can end one condition affecting it (blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned, or diseased). If it's an enemy, it takes radiant damage equal to your Wisdom modifier + your ascetic level and must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier) or be blinded until the end of your next turn.
- **Ember Step** (1 Flow, on your turn). You teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see, leaving a trail of harmless sacred flame. Each ally you pass within 5 feet of during this teleportation gains temporary hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier and has their speed increased by 10 feet until the end of their next turn.

**Inner Flame (1st Level)**. The sacred fire burns within you, sustained by your devotion — dimmer when your rhythm falters, but never fully extinguished. Even while Disrupted (Flow at -1 or lower), you shed dim light in a 10-foot radius; the flame gutters, but it does not go out, and Disrupted allies who huddle close still find their way by it. While you are Flowing (0 or higher Flow), you instead shed bright light in a 10-foot radius and dim light for an additional 10 feet, and you can suppress or restore this light as a bonus action; allies within your bright light have advantage on saving throws against being frightened. While Flawless (3+ Flow), the radius increases to 20 feet bright / 20 feet dim, and allies in your bright light also gain resistance to necrotic damage.

**Devotional Forms (3rd Level)**. You learn sacred combat techniques passed down through your order. Choose one of the following forms. You can switch forms as a bonus action:
- **Form of the Flickering Fire**: When you hit with a melee attack, you can move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks. This movement can occur before or after the attack.
- **Form of the Purifying Light**: When you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can choose to deal radiant damage instead of the weapon's normal damage type. If you do, you gain temporary hit points equal to half the damage dealt (minimum 1).
- **Form of the Eternal Vigil**: When an ally within 10 feet of you is hit by an attack, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage they take by an amount equal to your Wisdom modifier.

**Radiant Strikes (5th Level)**. Your unarmed strikes and simple weapons count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity. Once per turn when you hit a fiend or undead creature with a weapon attack, you deal an extra 1d6 radiant damage — the sacred flame answers such creatures whether or not you are Flowing. This damage increases to 2d6 at 11th level and 3d6 at 17th level.

**Beacon of Hope (6th Level)**. While Flowing (0+ Flow), allies within 10 feet of you gain a bonus to saving throws equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum +1). Additionally, when you use Purifying Touch to heal an ally, you can spend an additional Flow boost to also restore hit points to all allies within 10 feet equal to your Wisdom modifier.

**Shared Light**. Sacred flame doesn't burn any brighter for being borrowed twice. When a creature would benefit from more than one bonus of the same kind granted by divine radiance — your Beacon of Hope, a Paladin's aura, a Thaumaturge's blessing, or another Tammuzian's own flame — sacred flame does not combine with other sources of divine radiance; use the higher bonus only.

**Sacred Resilience (9th Level)**. You gain resistance to fire and radiant damage. While Flawless (3+ Flow), you have immunity to fire damage instead. Additionally, when you take fire or radiant damage from any source, you can use your reaction to gain 1 Flow boost as you channel the energy into divine purpose.

**Whirling Dervish (11th Level)**. When you use your action to Attack while Flowing (0+ Flow), you can forgo one of your attacks to spin in a sacred dance. Each creature of your choice within 5 feet must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier). On a failed save, a creature takes 3d8 radiant or fire damage (your choice) and is pushed 5 feet away from you. On a successful save, it takes half damage and isn't pushed. Allies within 10 feet of you when you use this feature regain hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier. You can forgo this healing; if you do, each fiend, undead, or corrupted creature that fails its save instead takes an extra 2d8 radiant or fire damage (your choice, made once for the whole burst).

**Pillar of Faith (14th Level)**. While at 2+ Flow, you emanate an aura of divine presence. Hostile creatures within 10 feet of you have disadvantage on attack rolls against your allies. Additionally, when you are reduced to 0 hit points while Flowing (0+ Flow), you can immediately spend all your remaining Flow (minimum 1) to drop to 1 hit point instead and shed blinding light; each hostile creature within 20 feet must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier) or be blinded until the end of their next turn. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

**Eternal Flame (18th Level)**. Your devotion has made you a beacon of sacred power. You gain the following benefits:
- Your Inner Flame radius permanently increases to 20 feet bright / 20 feet dim (30 feet / 30 feet while Flawless)
- Allies that start their turn in your bright light regain hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier
- When you use Purifying Touch on an ally, you can target up to three creatures within 5 feet of you simultaneously (spending Flow only once)
- **Miracle of the Sacred Flame** (once per long rest): When you or an ally you can see within 30 feet would be reduced to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to spend 3 Flow. The target instead drops to 1 hit point, regains hit points equal to twice your ascetic level, and is cured of all conditions and diseases. A pillar of radiant flame briefly illuminates the area, and all allies within 20 feet of the target regain hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier and gain advantage on their next attack roll or saving throw.

### Nityamar (nit-YAH-mar) Monks — Transcendence of Mortality

The Nityamar believe that perfection and mortality are fundamentally opposed. To achieve the ideal, one must shed the weakness of mortality altogether: hunger, pain, gravity, even the need for physical form. Through rigorous discipline, Nityamar monks forge a connection to Kwata (KWAH-tah), a perfect plane of existence where suffering, limitation, and imperfection do not exist. They are ascetic warriors who need no weapons or armor; their bodies and wills alone are sufficient. Each breakthrough in their practice brings them closer to Kwata, allowing them to impose its perfection upon the material world. The Nityamar seek out the world's cruelest extremes on purpose — the ocean floor, the smoldering heart of the Marram, the crushing depths of the Veldt, the polar caps at the bottom of the world — because only there does mortal weakness announce itself loudly enough to be renounced. Elves, Kelanari, Humans, Palcha, and Dramkhani count among their most devoted.

**Philosophy**: Perfection through denial of mortal limitations; power through connection to Kwata; freedom through transcendence.

**Flow Gain — Transcendent Discipline**. You gain 1 Flow boost when you succeed on a saving throw while you have half your hit points or fewer, when you reduce a creature to 0 hit points using only an unarmed strike, or when you hit with two or more unarmed strikes against the same creature on your turn.

**Flow Spend — Kwata Techniques**. You learn additional ways to spend Flow unique to your tradition:
- **Step Between Worlds** (1 Flow, on your turn). You partially phase into Kwata, becoming incorporeal until the end of your turn. You can move through creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain, taking 1d10 force damage if you end your turn inside an object. Opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage, and you have resistance to all damage except psychic and force damage until the end of your turn.
- **Perfect Strike** (2 Flow, immediate when you hit with an unarmed strike, or 3 Flow to apply both effects below). Your strike channels Kwata's absolute nature. Choose one of the following (or both, if you spent 3 Flow):
  - The attack automatically becomes a critical hit. You can also choose to deal force damage instead of bludgeoning damage.
  - The target must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier) or be stunned until the end of your next turn.

**Unarmed Mastery (1st Level)**. Your unarmed strikes are weapons of pure technique. Your unarmed strikes deal 1d6 bludgeoning damage. This die increases as you gain ascetic levels: to 1d8 at 5th level, 1d10 at 11th level, and 1d12 at 17th level. Additionally, when you use the Attack action with an unarmed strike on your turn, you can make one additional unarmed strike as a bonus action.

**Shed the Mortal Form (1st Level)**. You begin to transcend physical limitations. While you are wearing no armor and not wielding a shield:
- You can use your Dexterity modifier instead of Strength for grapple checks and Strength (Athletics) checks to escape grapples.
- When you are Flowing (0+ Flow), you take half damage from falling.
- While your Flow is +2 or higher, you gain a +2 bonus to AC (this stacks with Unarmored Defense) — full transcendence is a threshold, not a given, and it slips away the moment your rhythm does.
- At 6th level, your unarmed strikes count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity.

**Kwata Connection (3rd Level)**. Your bond with the perfect plane deepens. You gain the following benefits based on your current Flow:
- **At 1+ Flow**: You can walk on vertical surfaces and across liquids on your turn without falling during the move. If you end your turn on a vertical surface or liquid, you fall unless you have another means of staying aloft.
- **At 2+ Flow**: You have advantage on saving throws against being paralyzed, restrained, or stunned.
- **At 3+ Flow (Flawless)**: You can take the Dash or Disengage action as a bonus action on your turn.

**Flurry of Blows (5th Level)**. When you use your bonus action to make an unarmed strike (from Unarmed Mastery), you can spend 1 Flow to make two unarmed strikes instead of one.

**Deny Mortality (6th Level)**. Your connection to Kwata allows you to reject mortal weakness. You gain resistance to poison damage and advantage on saving throws against being poisoned or diseased. Additionally, you require only half as much food and water as normal, and you can hold your breath for a number of minutes equal to your Wisdom modifier × 10 (minimum 10 minutes).

**Ethereal Step (9th Level)**. You can briefly step fully into Kwata. As a bonus action, you can spend 2 Flow to cast *misty step* without components. When you use this feature, you become invisible until the start of your next turn or until you attack or cast a spell. Additionally, when you use Step Between Worlds, you can spend an additional Flow boost to remain incorporeal until the start of your next turn instead of until the end of your current turn.

**Stillness of Kwata (11th Level)**. When you take damage while Flowing (0+ Flow), you can use your reaction to enter a state of perfect stillness, reducing the damage by an amount equal to your Wisdom modifier + your ascetic level. If you reduce the damage to 0, you gain 1 Flow boost as you channel the attack's energy into your connection to Kwata.

**Quivering Palm (13th Level)**. You learn to strike the boundary between life and death. When you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can spend 3 Flow to start imperceptible vibrations that last for a number of days equal to your ascetic level. The vibrations are harmless unless you use your action to end them. To do so, you and the target must be on the same plane of existence. When you use your action to end the vibrations, the target must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier). On a failed save, it is reduced to 0 hit points. On a successful save, it takes 10d10 force damage. You can have only one creature under the effect of this feature at a time. You can choose to end the vibrations harmlessly without using an action.

**Empty Body (17th Level)**. You can achieve near-total transcendence. As an action, you can spend 4 Flow to become invisible for 1 minute. During this time, you also have resistance to all damage except force damage. While invisible in this way, you can spend an additional Flow boost at the start of your turn to gain the benefits of Step Between Worlds until the start of your next turn without using your action.

**Oneness with Kwata (18th Level)**. You exist partially in both worlds simultaneously. You gain the following permanent benefits:
- Your unarmed strikes deal force damage instead of bludgeoning damage, representing your connection to Kwata's perfect essence
- You can use Step Between Worlds without spending Flow, but you can do so only once per turn
- When you use Flurry of Blows, you can make three unarmed strikes instead of two (spending 1 Flow as normal)
- **Perfect Liberation** (once per long rest): When you would be reduced to 0 hit points, you can choose to instead fully enter Kwata for 1 minute. While in Kwata, you are invisible, incorporeal, and immune to all damage, but you cannot affect the material world in any way — you can't attack, cast spells, interact with objects, or restore hit points to any creature. If anything would force you to deal damage to a creature or restore hit points to a creature while you are in Kwata, you are immediately drawn back to the material plane into the nearest unoccupied space you can see, and that effect resolves normally once you return. Otherwise, you can move at your normal speed, passing through creatures and objects freely, and you regain hit points equal to your Wisdom modifier at the start of each of your turns while in Kwata. You can return to the material plane voluntarily, in an unoccupied space you can see, as a bonus action. When you return, you gain the benefits of being Flawless (3+ Flow) for 1 minute, regardless of your actual Flow.

### Nehushtani (neh-hoosh-TAH-nee) — The Adaptive Mirror

The Nehushtani believe that perfection cannot be found in a single form or philosophy, but in the ability to become anything required by the moment. They are combat analysts who study their enemies, steal their strengths, and turn their powers against them. Through careful observation and precise mimicry, Nehushtani ascetics learn to counter any threat, exploit any weakness, and adapt to any challenge. They are mirrors that reflect and counter their enemies, thieves who borrow power, and chameleons who become what the situation demands.

**Adaptation, Not Annihilation.** A Nehushtani doesn't burn magic out of the world the way a Varnic Knight's counter-glyphs do, and doesn't spin illusions of their own the way a Purple Mage does; they borrow, echo, and repurpose what already exists in front of them. Where a Varnic Knight ends a spell for good, a Nehushtani merely turns it, briefly, into something they can use themselves — and only after they've studied the caster closely enough to do so. A distinction that matters greatly to anyone trying to counter one.

**Philosophy**: Perfection through adaptability; power through understanding your enemy; mastery through becoming the answer to any threat.

**Flow Gain — Analytical Combat**. You gain 1 Flow boost when you:
- Successfully use a reaction to counter, mitigate, or redirect an enemy action (such as Flowing Counter, Mirror the Working, or Reflected Power)
- Succeed on a saving throw against a spell or magical effect
- Hit a creature with Adaptive Strike and learn information about it
- Successfully use a stolen or mimicked ability

**Flow Spend — Mirror Techniques**. You learn additional ways to spend Flow unique to your tradition:
- **Mirror the Working** (2 Flow, reaction when a creature you have studied with Combat Analysis this combat casts a spell within 60 feet). You attempt to read the working closely enough to turn it aside. The creature must make a Charisma saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier). On a failed save, the spell fails and has no effect, and you learn a downgraded echo of it: you can cast that spell once, at 2 levels lower than the level it was cast at (minimum 1st level), using Wisdom as your spellcasting ability and requiring no components. You can hold echoes of several different spells at once; each echo can be cast once per long rest, independently of the others. Once you use this feature to attempt a Mirror, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
- **Exploit Weakness** (1 Flow, on your turn when you attack a creature you've hit with Adaptive Strike). You exploit what you've learned. Your attack has advantage, and on a hit, you deal extra damage equal to 1d6 for every piece of information you've learned about that creature through Adaptive Strike, to a maximum number of dice equal to your proficiency bonus.

**Thousand Faces (1st Level)**. You can perfectly mimic the appearance, voice, and mannerisms of any humanoid you have observed for at least 1 minute. This transformation lasts until you choose to end it (no action required) or until you are incapacitated. Creatures can attempt a Wisdom (Insight) check contested by your Charisma (Deception) check to see through your disguise if they have reason to be suspicious. While disguised as a specific individual, you have advantage on Charisma checks to impersonate them and on your first attack roll against a creature that believes you are the person you're impersonating.

**Combat Analysis (1st Level)**. You excel at identifying patterns and weaknesses in combat. You gain proficiency in Insight if you don't already have it. If you do, you gain expertise in it (double your proficiency bonus). Additionally, as a bonus action while Flowing (0+ Flow), you can study one creature you can see within 30 feet. Until the end of your next turn, you have advantage on attack rolls against that creature, and you learn its highest and lowest ability scores. Studying a creature this way is a prerequisite for Mirror the Working to work against it later in the same combat.

**Mimic and Master (3rd Level)**. When a creature you can see within 30 feet hits you or an ally with an attack, you learn the attack's damage type and any special properties (such as additional effects or conditions). Until the end of your next turn, your weapon attacks can deal that damage type instead of their normal damage. Additionally, if the attack had a special property (such as knocking prone, causing a condition, or dealing extra damage on a failed save), you can spend 1 Flow when you hit with a weapon attack to apply that same property to your attack (the target makes the same saving throw with the same DC, or automatically suffers the effect if no save was allowed).

**Adaptive Strike (5th Level)**. Once per turn when you hit a creature with a weapon attack while Flowing (0+ Flow), you can adapt your strike to exploit weakness. You deal an extra 1d6 damage, and you learn one piece of information about the target: its current hit points, one damage resistance or immunity, one damage vulnerability, or one condition immunity. Each time you hit the same creature with Adaptive Strike, you learn an additional piece of information. This extra damage increases to 2d6 at 11th level and 3d6 at 17th level.

**Tactical Versatility (6th Level)**. Your adaptability grants you access to diverse techniques. You learn three techniques of your choice from the Fighter's technique catalog (see Fighter, Combat Techniques) at Familiar rank, treating your Wisdom modifier as your ability modifier for their effects and any Exertion-gated Improvised Use. You don't gain Exertion from this feature; if a technique would normally cost Exertion to use untrained, you instead spend 1 Flow.

**Reflected Power (9th Level)**. When you are targeted by a spell that targets only you (whether it requires an attack roll or saving throw), you can use your reaction to attempt to reflect it back at the caster. Make a Wisdom saving throw against the spell's save DC (or against DC 10 + spell level if it requires an attack roll). On a success, you take no damage or effect from the spell, and you can redirect it to target the caster or another creature within range, using the caster's spell attack bonus or save DC. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Adaptive Defense (11th Level)**. When you succeed on a saving throw against a spell or magical effect while Flowing (0+ Flow), you gain temporary hit points equal to twice your Wisdom modifier. Additionally, you gain resistance to the spell's damage type (if any) until the end of your next turn. If you take damage of that type before the resistance ends, you gain 1 Flow boost.

**Siphon Power (13th Level)**. You can temporarily rob enemies of their finest technique. As an action while Flowing (0+ Flow), you target one creature within 30 feet that you can see. It must make a Charisma saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Wisdom modifier). On a failed save, choose one martial technique or innate, non-spell trait you have witnessed the creature use this combat — a fighter-style maneuver, a monstrous natural weapon, a breath weapon, or another innate trait that isn't a spell, spell slot, or legendary action. For the next 10 minutes, the creature cannot use that technique or trait, and you gain the ability to use it once, replicating its effects (using your own ability scores and proficiency bonus, and its original saving throw DC formula if it forces one). You can only have one siphoned ability at a time. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

**Reactive Superiority (14th Level)**. Your analytical mind processes threats faster than others can react. You can take one additional reaction each round, for two reactions total when combined with Reactive Mastery. Additionally, when you use a reaction to successfully counter, mitigate, or avoid an attack or spell (such as causing an attack to miss, succeeding on a save, or using Reflected Power or Mirror the Working), you can move up to 15 feet without provoking opportunity attacks as part of that reaction.

**Master Thief (17th Level)**. Your power-stealing abilities improve dramatically. You can attempt Mirror the Working twice before needing a short or long rest, and its downgraded echoes are cast at only 1 level lower than the spell's original casting level (minimum 1st) instead of 2. Your Siphon Power can now copy two techniques or traits at once (from the same creature, or from two different creatures you've analyzed this combat), and you can use each siphoned ability twice before it returns to its source.

**Perfect Adaptation (18th Level)**. You become the ultimate mirror and counter-fighter. You gain the following benefits:
- Your Wisdom score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22.
- Whenever you gain 1 Flow boost, you can choose to gain 2 Flow boosts instead. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.
- Choose one martial technique or innate non-spell trait you have copied with Siphon Power; you retain it permanently instead of losing it after 10 minutes. You can change which ability is stored this way whenever you use Siphon Power again.
- **The Living Weapon** (once per long rest): As an action, you can spend 3 Flow to enter a state of perfect adaptation for 1 minute. While in this state, you gain the following benefits:
  - You can use Mirror the Working and Reflected Power without expending a limited use.
  - When you use Mirror the Working or Reflected Power, you don't need to spend Flow.
  - Whenever a creature within 60 feet uses a spell or a martial technique or innate trait, you immediately learn what it is and gain advantage on any saving throws against it.
  - Your Adaptive Strike bonus damage increases by 2d6.
  - You gain a +2 bonus to AC and saving throws.

  When this state ends, you retain all information you learned about creatures during it, and any ability siphoned with Siphon Power during this state can be used one additional time.

### Khalsari (kahl-SAH-ree) — Brotherhood of the Bound Name

The Khalsari hold that perfection is the perfect keeping of a single vow: the Bound Name, sworn once at initiation and renewed every dawn, together with the fivefold discipline that flows from it. They read the Bound Name as both covenant and code — half psalm, half statute — and they measure a life's worth by how faithfully its clauses are kept, not by how loudly its victories are sung. A Khalsari would rather talk a fight down than start one, but once a blade is drawn against the innocent, they hold the line without flinching and without cruelty. Where the Yargharu ride to punish the guilty after the fact, and the Pillars of Kalindor bind commerce to precedent, the Khalsari keep a narrower charge: the border road itself, and whoever walks it in good faith.

Khalsari cloisters sit at the world's thresholds — remote outposts near the Concord's treaty-roads, the approaches to Khurz-Marn, the trade courts ringing Kalindor — guardians posted at the gate rather than scholars shelved in a library. A Khalsari brother has likely never set foot in Ostro's Great Library, and would eye with mild suspicion anyone who claims to know a border only from having read about it. Humans, Orcs, Dwarves, and Halflings are drawn to the discipline in roughly equal measure; border justice, it turns out, is nobody's exclusive inheritance.

**Philosophy**: Righteousness through the perfect keeping of the Bound Name; strength through restraint offered before force; perfection through a fivefold discipline that never asks more of you than it asks of itself.

**Flow Gain — Brotherhood's Vigil**. You gain 1 Flow boost when you:
- Prevent an ally from dropping below half their hit points (such as by healing them, granting temporary hit points, or reducing damage they would take)
- Are the last conscious member of your party still standing in combat (once per combat)
- Complete a declared cadence of your Fivefold Discipline (once per turn, even if more than one cadence completes)

**Flow Spend — Guardian's Rites**. You learn additional ways to spend Flow unique to your tradition:
- **Interpose** (1 Flow, reaction when an ally within 5 feet is hit by an attack). You place yourself between your ally and the attack. The attack targets you instead, and you gain resistance to the attack's damage.
- **Bar the Way** (1 Flow, when you hit with a weapon attack). Name one creature within 30 feet as the one your target must not approach. The target can't willingly move within 5 feet of that creature until the start of your next turn.

**Vow of the Bound (1st Level)**. You have sworn the Bound Name and hold to it before every stranger, judge, and blade. You gain the following benefits:
- You can use Charisma instead of Wisdom for your Unarmored Defense calculation (AC = 10 + Dexterity modifier + Charisma modifier).
- You gain proficiency in Persuasion and Insight. If you already have proficiency in either skill, you gain expertise in it instead.
- When you make an attack roll against a creature that has not attacked, forced a saving throw upon, or dealt damage to you or an ally since this combat began, you lose 1 Flow boost (potentially becoming Disrupted) — the Bound Name forbids striking first at the innocent.

**Mark of the Bound (1st Level)**. As a bonus action while Flowing (0+ Flow), you invoke the Bound Name against one creature you can see within 30 feet, marking it until the start of your next turn. While a creature is Marked by you:
- It has disadvantage on attack rolls against creatures other than you.
- Allies have advantage on Wisdom (Insight) checks made against it.
- When it hits you with an attack, you gain 1 Flow boost (once per turn).

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1), and you regain all expended uses when you finish a short or long rest.

**Fivefold Discipline (3rd Level)**. Each dawn, you renew five vows: the Vow of the Name, the Vow of the Blade, the Vow of Service, the Vow of Purity, and the Vow of Witness. As a free action on your turn (once per turn), you can declare one vow's cadence — a recitation, a stance, or a deliberate restraint. If you complete that vow's condition before the start of your next turn, you gain 1 Flow boost (see Flow Gain — Brotherhood's Vigil):
- **Name**: Declare by reciting the Bound Name aloud (no language component required). Complete by ending your turn within 5 feet of an ally.
- **Blade**: Declare by resting your weapon's edge toward your Mark. Complete by hitting a creature Marked by you with a weapon attack.
- **Service**: Declare by turning to face an endangered ally. Complete by using Interpose, or by otherwise preventing an ally from taking damage.
- **Purity**: Declare by lowering your weapon. Complete by ending your turn without having attacked a creature that hasn't threatened you or an ally this combat.
- **Witness**: Declare by marking what you see. Complete when an ally within 30 feet of you succeeds on a saving throw.

**Stay Your Hand (5th Level)**. As a reaction, when a creature you can see within 30 feet makes an attack roll or an ability check, you invoke the Bound Name to stay its hand: that roll is made with disadvantage. If the roll was an attack roll and it misses because of this disadvantage, and the attacker is within your reach, you can immediately make one melee weapon attack against it as part of the same reaction.

**Brotherhood's Aura (6th Level)**. While you are Flowing, you radiate the Brotherhood's steadfastness. Choose one of the following benefits for allies within 10 feet of you; you can change your choice as a bonus action:
- Allies gain a +1 bonus to saving throws.
- Allies gain a +1 bonus to AC.

Additionally, when you are reduced to half your hit points or fewer, you gain temporary hit points equal to your Charisma modifier + your ascetic level. Once you gain these temporary hit points this way, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

**Testament of the Blade (9th Level)**. Once per turn when you hit a creature Marked by your Mark of the Bound with a weapon attack, you deal extra radiant damage equal to your proficiency bonus, and that creature has disadvantage on the next saving throw it makes before the start of your next turn.

**Steadfast Line (11th Level)**. Your stance holds a border that isn't crossed twice. While you are Flowing, you can't be moved against your will or knocked prone by an effect created by a creature within 10 feet of you, and difficult terrain doesn't cost you extra movement. Additionally, once per long rest, when you use Interpose, you can protect two allies struck by the same attack instead of one.

**Unbreakable Vow (13th Level)**. Your discipline steels your mind against every trial. You gain proficiency in all saving throws. Additionally, when you fail a saving throw while Flowing (0+ Flow), you can choose to succeed instead. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

**Sentence of the Bound (14th Level)**. When you hit a creature that is hindered by one or more of your abilities (such as Mark of the Bound, Testament of the Blade, or Bar the Way), you can force a decisive reckoning. The creature must succeed on a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier) or be stunned until the end of your next turn and take extra radiant damage equal to your ascetic level. On a successful save, it takes half the extra damage and isn't stunned. Once you use this feature, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

**Guardian of the Gate (17th Level)**. Your vigilance covers more ground than one gate alone. You can Mark up to two creatures simultaneously with Mark of the Bound. Additionally, you can use Mark of the Bound as a reaction when a creature enters melee range of you or an ally within 30 feet, instead of only as a bonus action; when you do, you can Mark that creature or any other creature within 30 feet of you.

**The Unbending Line (18th Level)**. You have become the wall the Brotherhood swears by. You gain the following benefits:
- Your Charisma score increases by 2, to a maximum of 22.
- You can use Mark of the Bound without expending a limited use.
- Your Brotherhood's Aura's range increases to 15 feet.
- **Guardian's Intercession** (once per long rest): When an ally within 30 feet of you that you can see would be reduced to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of that ally. The triggering attack or effect strikes you instead, and you gain resistance to that instance of damage.

## Conclusion

The Ascetic class offers four distinct paths to perfection, each reflecting different philosophical traditions and combat styles. Whether you choose to be a radiant healer dancing through combat, a transcendent warrior phasing between planes, an adaptive mirror reflecting all forms, or a bound guardian holding a border others cannot, your journey toward perfection will be defined by your Flow — the measure of how closely you approach the ideal with each action you take.
